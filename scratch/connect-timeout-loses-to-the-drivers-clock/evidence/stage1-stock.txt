  server=silent   SKEW_RATE=1.0   bound=2s  monotonic elapsed= 2.0160s   PROMISE KEPT   DatabaseConnectTimeout

      libpq read the realtime clock 5 times; 4 before the long wait:
        t =     0.0000 ms
        t =     0.0202 ms   (+0.0202 ms)
        t =     0.0261 ms   (+0.0059 ms)
        t =     0.0522 ms   (+0.0261 ms)
        t =  2001.0542 ms   (+2001.0021 ms)   <- poll() returned; wait is over
      exposure window = 0.0522 ms. A forward realtime jump only shortens the wait if it lands in there,
      because the last read is where the remaining budget becomes a relative poll() timeout -- and the kernel measures THAT on monotonic.
